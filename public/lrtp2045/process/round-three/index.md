# Phase 3: Final Plan Adoption & Documentation

## Goal

Incorporate public input, finalize the plan and conformity documentation, and obtain formal MPO adoption.

## Activities include:

* Review public comments and document responses.
* Revise and finalize PLAN2050 and the Transportation Conformity Report.
* Document the public involvement process and how feedback was addressed.
* Present the final plan to the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC).
* Present the final plan and adoption resolution to the MPO Policy Committee for formal approval.
* Publish and distribute the adopted PLAN2050.

## What is produced:

An adopted PLAN2050, final Transportation Conformity Report, and complete documentation of the public involvement process.

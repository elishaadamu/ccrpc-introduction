# Phase 1: Visioning, Goals & Needs Assessment

## Goal

Establish a shared regional vision, identify transportation needs and priorities, and develop the goals and performance measures that will guide PLAN2050.

## Process

TCAMPO used online surveys, social media, community outreach, stakeholder coordination, and interactive engagement to gather input on transportation conditions, safety, accessibility, congestion, transit, bicycle and pedestrian needs, and funding priorities.

## What is Produced

A clear set of community priorities, transportation needs, goals, objectives, and performance measures to guide project evaluation and prioritization in later phases.

## Lessons Learned

PLAN2045 demonstrated the value of combining traditional and digital outreach. Short, mobile-friendly surveys, multiple communication channels, and targeted outreach can broaden participation, particularly among underserved communities. TCAMPO will continue to expand these approaches in PLAN2050.

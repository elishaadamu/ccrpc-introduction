# Phase 2: Project Prioritization & Draft Conformity Report

## Goal

Develop and prioritize transportation projects and strategies, evaluate funding and air quality impacts, and present the draft PLAN2050 Conformity Report for public review.

## Activities include:

* Develop, evaluate, and prioritize proposed projects and strategies.
* Develop a fiscally constrained project list based on projected revenues.
* Analyze transportation investments, performance, and air quality conformity.
* Prepare the Draft PLAN2050 Conformity Report.
* Conduct a 30-day public comment period and public meetings.
* Gather feedback to refine the draft recommendations.

## What is produced:

A prioritized, fiscally constrained project list, draft PLAN2050, and Draft Conformity Report.

See [Conformity Report](https://craterpdc.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/3_Draft-RCA-Richmond-Area-Amended-FY27-30-TIP-and-2050-LRTP.pdf).
